## Timeline

| Milestone | Date | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|
| Launch as appear.in | 2013-01-01 | Browser-based video meetings with no downloads required |
| Rebranded to Whereby | 2019-09-01 | Company renamed from appear.in to Whereby |
| Whereby Embedded launch | 2020-10-01 | API for embedding video calls in third-party apps |
| Breakout Groups added | 2021-06-01 | Support for splitting meetings into smaller groups |

## Pricing

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Free | $0/mo | 4 attendees, 30-min limit, 1 room |
| Pro | $10.99/mo | 100 attendees, unlimited duration, local recording |
| Business | $13.99/host/mo | 200 attendees, minimum 3 hosts |
