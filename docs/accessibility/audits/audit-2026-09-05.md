## Methodology

The audit used the monorepo `a11y-audit` v3 workflow, Node.js 26.7.0,
Puppeteer 25.10.0, headless Chromium, and axe-core 4.12.1 at 1280 by 800.
The first production-targeted sample found shared landmark, region, contrast,
and color-only-link defects. Source templates were corrected, the site was
rebuilt, and a canonical-to-loopback rewrite forced final checks against the
local artifact. Lighthouse was skipped because it is a separate optional lane.
